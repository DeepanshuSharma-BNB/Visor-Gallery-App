A little gallery,
right in your glasses.

Pick photos from your phone, pair with the code shown on your glasses, and tap a thumbnail to show it instantly. Nothing is automatically uploaded from your gallery. Only pictures you choose are sent.

01 / Pair your glasses

Open Visor Gallery on your glasses. Enter the 12-character code that appears on the display.

02 / Choose photos

Select up to 60 pictures. They are resized on your phone and transferred for display only after you pair.

No photos selected

03 / Play a video

Transfer a local MP4 or WebM video (up to 25 MB) over a direct encrypted WebRTC connection, if supported on both devices. No video file is uploaded to GitHub.

Waiting for peer-to-peer connection...

Or paste a supported public video URL. Direct MP4/WebM, YouTube and Vimeo are supported where embedding is permitted. X posts and other pages are experimental.

The video website itself may learn your glasses' IP address when loaded. Site restrictions, cookies, DRM, and unsupported codecs cannot be bypassed.

Tip: keep this browser tab open while viewing. Locking your phone may pause the connection. The shared public test relay is for experiments, not highly sensitive pictures.
